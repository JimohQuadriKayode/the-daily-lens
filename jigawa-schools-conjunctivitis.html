Jigawa Schools Urged to Strengthen Hygiene Measures Amid Reported Conjunctivitis Outbreak

The Jigawa State Senior Secondary Education Board has urged schools to strengthen hygiene, awareness and early reporting measures following a reported outbreak of conjunctivitis as students prepare to resume school.

Published by The Daily Lens • News & Health • 29 August 2026
Schools are being encouraged to strengthen hygiene and health awareness as students prepare to resume.

The Jigawa State Senior Secondary Education Board (JSSSEB) has called on principals and school directors to strengthen preventive measures following a reported outbreak of conjunctivitis, commonly known as Apollo, across Jigawa State.

The call comes as students prepare to resume school, with the Board highlighting concerns about the highly contagious nature of the infection and the potential for transmission in school environments.

Schools Urged to Strengthen Prevention

In a notification dated 29 August 2026, the Board drew the attention of school authorities to the reported situation and urged them to put appropriate preventive measures in place.

According to the notification, boarding schools may face increased transmission risks because students live and interact closely with one another.

“Early intervention will help minimize the risk of further spread and protect the health and well-being of both students and staff.”

Hygiene and Awareness Measures

The Board advised schools to strengthen hygiene practices and increase awareness among students and staff.

Schools were also encouraged to identify possible cases early and take appropriate measures to reduce contact transmission.

CONJUNCTIVITIS (APOLLO) — Redness • Irritation • Watery eyes.

What Schools, Students and Staff Can Do

Schools can support prevention by encouraging regular hand hygiene, maintaining clean shared environments and promoting awareness about infectious illnesses.

Students and staff should avoid unnecessary close contact when they are unwell and should seek appropriate medical advice when symptoms develop or become concerning.

Personal items that may come into contact with bodily fluids should not be shared where doing so could increase the risk of infection.

Health reminder: Conjunctivitis can have different causes. Anyone experiencing symptoms should seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional, particularly when symptoms are severe, persistent or affecting vision.

Protecting Students and Staff

The Board said early intervention would help minimize the risk of further spread and protect the health and well-being of both students and staff.

As schools prepare for resumption, cooperation among school administrators, teachers, students, parents and health professionals will be important in promoting safer school environments.

About the Notification

The notification was addressed to principals and school directors and was signed by Mansur Ubale, Deputy Director, Schools Services, for the Executive Chairman of the Jigawa State Senior Secondary Education Board.

The notification cited reference number JSSSEB/ADM/26/158/VOL.1/03 and was dated 29 August 2026.

Source and editorial note
